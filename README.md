# -CS-330-Computational-Graphics-and-Visualization
    CS 330 Computational Graphics and Visualization Portfolio
How do I approach designing software?

I approach software design by first understanding what the final program needs to accomplish and then breaking the project into smaller parts. During my 3D scene project, I learned how important it is to plan the objects, shapes, textures, lighting, and camera movement before trying to put everything together. One of the biggest design skills I developed was learning how basic 3D shapes can be transformed and combined to represent more complex real-world objects.

For this project, I worked through the scene gradually and made adjustments as I saw how each object appeared when rendered. This process taught me that software design does not have to be perfect on the first attempt. Testing individual components and making changes based on the results is a strategy I can continue using in future projects.

How do I approach developing programs?

My development approach involves building programs in smaller sections, testing frequently, and fixing problems before moving on. While developing my 3D scene, I gained more experience with C++, OpenGL, transformations, textures, lighting, and camera controls. Instead of trying to create the entire scene at once, I was able to develop and improve different elements throughout the course milestones.

Iteration played a major role in completing my project. I repeatedly adjusted the position, rotation, and scale of objects until the scene more closely represented what I wanted to create. I also improved the appearance of the scene by applying textures and lighting. Throughout the milestones, my approach became more organized because I became more comfortable separating different responsibilities in my code and understanding how changes to one part of the program affected the complete scene.

How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?

Computer science gives me the ability to take an idea and turn it into a working application. Computational graphics gave me new experience with concepts such as coordinate systems, transformations, textures, lighting, cameras, and interactive controls. These skills will be useful throughout my educational pathway because they strengthened my understanding of C++ programming and showed me how mathematical concepts can be applied directly to software development.

Professionally, this course also strengthened my problem-solving and debugging skills. Even if I do not specialize in computer graphics, the development process used in this project can apply to many areas of software engineering. Building the 3D scene required planning, testing, debugging, organization, and continuous improvement. These are skills I can apply when developing applications and working on larger software projects in my future career.
